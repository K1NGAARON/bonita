Discover activities in Moraira

Activities

Golfing

Golf is a very popular sport on the Costa Blanca. Around Moraira you will find several golf courses. Whether you choose 9 or 18 holes, with or without sea views, the possibilities are endless. The nearest courses are Club de Golf Ifach, Club de Golf Jávea and Club de Golf Altea.

Padelles

In the centre of Moraira you will find several padel courts. This popular sport is ideal for both beginners and advanced players. Come and enjoy an active and fun experience during your holiday at La Bonita!

Cycling

Moraira is a cyclists' paradise. With its beautiful routes along the coast and through the mountainous interior, it offers something for everyone. There are bikes for rent at several places in Moraira.

Moraira eBikes Moraira cycling Viator

Diving

Discover the underwater world on the Costa Blanca! There are more than 25 dive sites to explore in Moraira, Jávea, Benitachell and Calpe. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced diver, there are several diving schools nearby to guide your adventure.

Sailing

Boating along Moraira's coast is an unforgettable experience. Explore the coastline, discover hidden coves, and enjoy a relaxing day on the water. For a day at sea, you can easily rent a boat from local rental companies.

Jet ski

Experience an adrenaline rush on the Mediterranean with an exciting jet ski excursion. Explore the beautiful coastline and hidden caves as you skim over the water. Local jet ski rental companies offer guided tours and rentals, ideal for beginners and experienced water sports enthusiasts alike.

Manawa

Kayaking

Explore the beautiful coastline of La Cala de la Granadella, located in the southernmost part of Jávea. With a kayak, you can paddle along the breathtaking coastline, navigate through picturesque hidden coves and even explore mysterious sea caves.

Sailing school and water sports centre

Explore the water sports opportunities in Moraira with lessons at the local sailing school, perfect for both beginners and experienced sailors. You can also hire a SUP or kayak from the water sports centre to explore the coastline at your own pace and enjoy the beautiful surroundings.

Barcos & Cosas

Karting

Experience the thrill of karting at AV-Karting in Moraira! Here you will find different karts with varying speeds, including duo karts for an adventurous ride together. Ideal for a fun, adventurous day during your stay at La Bonita.

Playa de L'Ampollo - Moraira

Playa de L'Ampolla is a beautiful beach in Moraira, known for its crystal-clear waters and fine sand. The beach is ideal for families and there is a nice beach bar where you can also rent sunbeds and umbrellas. Parking is available next to the beach for your car.

Playa De El Portet - Moraira

Playa de El Portet is a small cove with a beautiful sandy beach, located in charming Moraira. With its calm waters and breathtaking views, this idyllic location is perfect for a relaxing day by the sea.

Vineyards - LLiber

Visit the vineyards of Pepe Mendoza Casa Agrícola in Llíber, where passion for winemaking comes alive. This idyllic location in the Jalón Valley, near Moraira, offers an unforgettable experience for wine lovers.

Cala del Moraig - Benitachell

Cala del Moraig, located in Benitachell near Moraira, is a breathtaking cove surrounded by impressive cliffs. This hidden paradise offers crystal-clear waters, ideal for snorkelling and diving. From the rock Cova dels Arcs, you can discover an underground river while diving.

Playa Arenal - Javea

Playa Arenal in Jávea, near Moraira, is a beautiful sandy beach perfect for a relaxing day by the sea. With its soft sand and calm, shallow waters, it is ideal for families and swimmers. Along the lively promenade you will find several cafés, restaurants and local shops.

Cap de Sant Antoni - Javea

Cap de Sant Antoni in Jávea, near Moraira, offers breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea. This nature reserve is perfect for hikers and nature lovers, with beautiful cliffs, several hiking trails and rich flora and fauna. From the lighthouse, you have spectacular panoramic views of the coastline.

Los Molinos de la Plana de Javea

Discover the Los Molinos de la Plana de Jávea hiking trail, a historic gem in the Costa Blanca's natural beauty. This route takes you past old windmills and offers breathtaking views of the Mediterranean and the surrounding landscape.

Hiking trail - Benissa/Calpe

The coastal footpath between Benissa and Calpe, also known as Paseo Ecológico, is a true paradise for hiking enthusiasts. This route offers breathtaking views of the Mediterranean Sea and the rugged coastline with beaches and coves.

Playa de la fustera - Benissa

Playa de la Fustera in Benissa, near Moraira, is a charming sandy beach on a small bay surrounded by lush nature. This idyllic beach offers crystal-clear waters, ideal for swimming and snorkelling. You can walk along this beautiful bay via the footpath.

Old-Altea

Old Altea, located near Moraira, is an authentic town full of charm and history. Stroll through the narrow, cobbled streets, admire the whitewashed houses and enjoy the beautiful views over the Mediterranean Sea. Visit the iconic church of La Mare de Déu del Consol with its striking blue domes.

Reservoir - Guedelest

Guadalest reservoir is a beautiful, turquoise oasis surrounded by imposing mountains. This serene lake offers a spectacular contrast to the historic charm of the nearby village and castle of Guadalest.

Waterfalls - Algar

Visit the waterfalls of Algar. This natural oasis offers crystal clear natural ‘pools’, lush vegetation and refreshing waterfalls, perfect for a day of swimming and relaxing in nature.

Forat De Bernia

The Forat de Bernia is a legendary hiking route that takes adventurers on an exhilarating journey through the natural splendour of the Costa Blanca. This challenging route offers breathtaking mountain and sea views as you navigate through ancient tunnels and along steep paths.
